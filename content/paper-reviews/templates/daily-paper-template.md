## The contribution in one minute

Write an independent summary from the original paper. Do not copy the discovery service's generated summary.

## Method and evidence

Explain the design, training, data, evaluation setup, and results. Include source figures and tables with captions.

## Limits and engineering implications

## Terminology

## References and figure credits
